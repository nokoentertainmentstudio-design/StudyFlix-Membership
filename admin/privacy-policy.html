Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains how StudyFlix collects, uses, stores and protects personal information.

Last Updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

StudyFlix ("StudyFlix", "we", "us" or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, protect and otherwise process personal information when you use the StudyFlix website, membership services and related features.

StudyFlix aims to process personal information responsibly and in accordance with applicable South African data protection legislation, including the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA), where applicable.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use StudyFlix, we may collect information such as:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Telephone or cellphone number
  • School name
  • School location
  • School code
  • Membership information
  • Membership application information
  • Account authentication information
  • Information submitted through forms on the website
  • Information necessary to verify membership status

We aim to collect information that is relevant and necessary for the specific purposes for which it is processed.

3. How We Collect Information

Information may be collected when you:

  • Create a StudyFlix account
  • Submit a membership application
  • Update your membership information
  • Contact StudyFlix
  • Use membership verification services
  • Interact with StudyFlix features and services

4. How We Use Your Information

StudyFlix may use personal information for purposes including:

  • Creating and managing user accounts
  • Processing membership applications
  • Reviewing and approving memberships
  • Maintaining membership records
  • Providing membership services
  • Verifying whether a membership is active
  • Communicating with members
  • Maintaining the security of the platform
  • Preventing fraud, abuse and unauthorised access
  • Improving StudyFlix services
  • Complying with applicable legal obligations

5. Membership Verification

StudyFlix provides a membership verification feature that may allow authorised partners to verify whether a StudyFlix membership is active.

Important:

The verification service is designed to provide only the information reasonably necessary to confirm membership. It is not intended to provide partners with unrestricted access to a member's private account information.

A membership verification QR code may direct a partner to the StudyFlix verification service. The QR code is intended to assist with verification and should not contain unnecessary personal information.

6. Firebase and Third-Party Services

StudyFlix uses Firebase services provided by Google to support features such as user authentication and database functionality.

Information processed through these services may be stored and processed using the infrastructure provided by the relevant service provider.

We take reasonable steps to configure and use these services appropriately and to protect personal information against unauthorised access.

7. Access to Personal Information

Access to member information is restricted according to the role and function of the person accessing the system.

For example, administrative access may be required to review membership applications, while partner verification is designed to expose only limited information necessary for membership verification.

8. Information Security

StudyFlix takes reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against loss, misuse, unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration or destruction.

No internet-based system can guarantee absolute security. Users should therefore also take reasonable steps to protect their account credentials and devices.

9. Account Security

Users are responsible for keeping their account credentials confidential.

You should not share your password with another person. If you believe that your account has been accessed without permission, you should contact StudyFlix as soon as possible.

10. Data Retention

StudyFlix may retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, manage memberships, maintain appropriate records, resolve disputes, comply with legal obligations and protect the legitimate interests of the platform.

When information is no longer required and there is no lawful reason to retain it, reasonable steps may be taken to delete, destroy or anonymise it.

11. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law and any relevant limitations, you may have rights concerning your personal information, including the right to:

  • Request access to personal information held about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion where legally applicable
  • Object to certain forms of processing where applicable
  • Raise a complaint regarding the processing of your information

Requests may be subject to verification and applicable legal requirements.

12. Learners Information

StudyFlix may provide services intended for school learners.We recognise that information relating to children requires particular care.

Where applicable, StudyFlix will process information relating to children in accordance with applicable legal requirements and appropriate safeguards.

Users should provide only information that is necessary for the intended StudyFlix service.

13. Cookies and Similar Technologies

StudyFlix may use cookies, browser storage or similar technologies where necessary for website functionality, authentication, security or improving the user experience.

Your browser may provide controls for managing certain cookies and storage technologies.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

StudyFlix may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the platform, services, technology or legal requirements.

The updated version will be published on this page together with the date on which it was last updated.

15. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or the processing of your personal information, please contact StudyFlix.

16. Complaints

If you are not satisfied with how your personal information has been handled, you may have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa, subject to the applicable legal process.
